Good to know

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before your first clean. Still curious? Our team replies within a few hours.

Is Greenland safe to use myself, or do I need a technician?
Totally safe to use yourself — that's the whole point. No dismantling, no special tools and no technician required. Just spray the foaming cleanser onto the coils and fins, wait about 60 seconds, rinse, and you're done. Most people finish in under five minutes.
Will it damage my air conditioner's coils or fins?
No. Greenland is pH-balanced and formulated specifically for the delicate aluminium fins and copper coils used in modern AC units. It's powerful on grime yet gentle on metal, plastic and rubber.
How often should I clean my AC with Greenland?
For most homes, a deep clean every 6–8 weeks keeps air fresh and efficiency high. If anyone in your home has allergies, every 4 weeks is ideal.
Is it safe around children, pets and houseplants?
Yes. The formula is plant-based and fume-free with no harsh chemical odour, so it's safe to use around babies, pets and sensitive plants.
