Good to know

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before your first clean. Still curious? Our team replies within a few hours.

Is Greenland safe to use myself, or do I need a technician?
Totally safe to use yourself. No dismantling, no special tools and no technician required. Spray onto the coils and fins, wait about 60 seconds, rinse, and you're done — usually under five minutes.
Will it damage coils, fins or surfaces?
No. Each formula is balanced for the material it's made for — powerful on grime yet gentle on metal, plastic and rubber.
How often should I clean?
For most homes, a deep clean every 6–8 weeks keeps things fresh and efficient. If anyone has allergies, every 4 weeks is ideal.
Is it safe around children, pets and plants?
Yes. The formula is plant-based and fume-free with no harsh chemical odour, so it's safe around babies, pets and sensitive plants.
